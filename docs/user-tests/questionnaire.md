# User test questionnaire

Three questions, used in both user tests (the radio cut, then the near-final cut), so the answers
can be compared. Asked after one play-through, before the intent is explained. Answers are written
down in the tester's own words. Shortened from ten questions to three (Wed 25 Nov) so the testers
have time to answer.

Before playing: "This is a draft of a short film I'm making. Please be honest; it helps me more
than compliments." (Test 1: "There are no pictures yet, just the voice and placeholder music.")

1. **In one sentence, what was the film about?**
   (Did they understand the story?)
2. **How did you feel at the end, and did it make you think of a holiday in your own family?**
   (The intent in DESIGN.md: feel happy, see that being there matters, think of their own family.)
3. **Was anything confusing, too slow, or did any Amharic word sound wrong?**
   (Problems that lead to storyboard changes.)

## Optional: one-question blind voice test (test 1)

The last line of the film is played in four voices labelled A, B, C and D, in random order; the key
is opened only afterwards. "Which one sounds most like natural Amharic?" This is how the film's
voice is chosen (decision 25).

## Afterwards (me)

Write each person's answers in DESIGN.md the same day, and for every problem note the storyboard
change it leads to, or why nothing changed.
