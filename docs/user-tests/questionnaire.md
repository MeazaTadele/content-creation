# User test questionnaire

Used for both user tests (the radio cut, then the near-final cut), so answers can be compared.
Questions are asked **in this order**, before I say what I intended, and answers are written down
in the tester's own words. Written from DESIGN.md (audience and intent).

## Before playing

- "This is a draft of a short film I'm making. There are no wrong answers. Please be honest; it
  helps me more than compliments."
- Test 1 only: "There are no pictures yet, just the voice and some placeholder music."
- Play it once, without pausing or explaining.

## Questions

**What they understood**

1. Tell me the story back in your own words. What happened?
2. Who is the story about? What do you know about each person?
3. Was there a moment where you got lost, or didn't understand? Where?

**What they felt**

4. How did you feel at the end?
5. Was there a moment that stood out? Which one, and why?
6. Was there a moment that felt too long, too slow, or boring? Where?

**The message (the intent)**

7. What do you think the film is saying? Is it saying anything about being there for people?
8. Did it make you think of a holiday in your own family? Which one? (Ask for a specific memory.)

**The voice and the language**

9. Did any Amharic word sound wrong, unclear or mispronounced? Which?
10. Did the voice sound natural? Did it fit the story?

**Test 2 only: picture and sound**

11. Did the pictures match what the voice was saying? Was anything confusing or wrong-looking?
12. Could you read the subtitles easily? Was the music ever too loud under the voice?

## Blind voice test (test 1, optional, about 2 minutes)

Two narration lines (shot 5, the turn, and shot 10, the last line) are played in four voices,
labelled A, B, C and D in random order. The key is kept separately and opened only afterwards.

For each line: "Which voice sounds most like natural Amharic? Which sounds least natural? Is any
word wrong?"

## Afterwards (me)

- Write down each person's answers the same day, in DESIGN.md, under the test.
- For every answer that points to a problem, note the storyboard change it leads to, or why I
  decided not to change anything.
